Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

1. Order Cancellations

We understand that plans can change. You can cancel your order under the following conditions:

  • Standard Items: Orders for regular items (breads, cookies, non-customized cakes) can be cancelled within 2 hours of order placement.
  • Customized / Special Orders: For customized cakes (with names, photos, or special designs), cancellations are only accepted if the order has not yet entered preparation. Once baking or decoration has begun, the order cannot be cancelled or refunded.
  • How to Cancel: To request a cancellation, please log in to your account, visit your order history and click "Cancel", or contact our support team immediately at +91 98765 43210.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are issued under the following circumstances:

  • The order is successfully cancelled within the permitted time windows described above.
  • We are unable to fulfill or deliver your order due to supply issues, severe weather conditions, or unforeseen operational limitations.
  • The delivered products have a verified structural defect or do not match the ordered specifications (photographic proof and instant reports to delivery personnel are required).

3. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery address provided by the customer during checkout.
  • The recipient is unavailable at the delivery address at the scheduled delivery time, or does not answer our delivery partner's phone calls.
  • Cancellations requested after the preparation of customized cakes has commenced or after the order has been dispatched.

4. Refund Processing & Timelines

Once your refund request is approved, we will initiate the refund back to your original payment source:

  • Refund Method: Approved refunds will be credited back automatically to the original online payment method (Debit Card, Credit Card, Netbanking, UPI, or Wallet) used at the time of purchase.
  • Processing Timeline: We process approved refunds internally within 24 to 48 hours. However, due to banking and payment gateway clearance protocols, it typically takes 5 to 7 business days for the refunded amount to reflect in your bank account or card statement.

5. Contact Support for Refunds

If you need to discuss an order cancellation or require assistance with a pending refund, please contact us at billing@sripadsbakery.com or call our helpline at +91 98765 43210.
